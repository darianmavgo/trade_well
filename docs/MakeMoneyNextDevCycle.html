MakeMoneyNextDevCycle

What is left before the backtested strategies can be trusted with real money. Written 2026-09-23 from docs/Review.md (2026-09-21) plus the work done since. Nothing here promises profit.

Reality checkNo engineering list gets you to steady daily profit. The biggest gap is whether the strategies make money live at all. Backtested edge on leveraged ETFs shrinks in live trading more often than not, and even a real edge comes with deep drawdowns.

1. Whether the strategies make money

GapWhy it matters
Zero slippage and commissiongld-decline, sig-voo-buy-tecl and sig-voo-buy-spxu were backtested with no costs. TECL, SPXU, TQQQ and SQQQ have real spreads and gap moves, so live results will come in below backtests.
OverfittingMany variants are built and the winners kept. A single 12-month in-sample/out-of-sample split does not protect against picking winners by luck.
Survivorship biasETF discovery only includes tickers active today, so leveraged ETFs that blew up never appear in the backtests.
No live-vs-backtest checkNothing compares what a strategy did live to what the simulator says it should have done. That report is the only thing that shows whether the edge is real.
Very new codeThe strategies, simulator and gating are about two weeks old. Weeks of live results are worth more than another backtest.

What to do, in order

  1. Deploy. Then keep everything in dry-run, or one share of a liquid ETF, for several weeks.
  2. Build the live-vs-backtest reconciliation report and read it weekly.
  3. Add the risk limits, alerts on all cron jobs, and the allowlist gate.
  4. Re-run the backtests with realistic costs and drop any strategy that fails.
  5. Scale up only what still works after that, using money you can afford to lose.
