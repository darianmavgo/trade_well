How to read it

  • Row 0 is the live stack, scored with the old coil/bounce tree rules (git commit 6d6b1b9), because the repo's pdd_tree and mara_tree rows now run the retrained 5-bucket tree.
  • Row 1 is the smallest change: the live stack plus one urbn streak. The edge is modest, about 8 points of held-out CAGR and 1.2 points of drawdown.
  • Rows 2 to 5 share a core (cls_tree, nxt, ttd, avgo, roku). They are variations on one idea, not five independent bets.
  • Held-out drawdowns of rows 2 to 5 (16.6% to 19.6%) are only slightly better than the deployed 19.7%. Their in-sample drawdown margin is much larger.

Caveats

The held-out year helped choose these stacks, so their held-out numbers are optimistic. They came from a search of about 4,000 stacks; 541 beat the deployed stack on all four numbers and 17 passed the liquidity filter. The simulator has no volume limit, so size matters in practice. Stacks with 400%+ in-sample CAGR leaned on small caps (dec, umac, esta, dsgn, ehgo) and were excluded. The trees cls, roiv, mdgl and ftai use the retrained models trained on the in-sample window only; trading them live needs one more train tree over all history.
