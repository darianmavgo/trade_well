Default service — operator pages

The link opens the GET page. Nothing trades until the form on that page is posted with its one-time token. POST /manage_exits with mode=live sells due positions at market. POST /exit_market with mode=live cancels working orders and sells long equity and ETF positions. POST /run_e2e_live places real orders. mode=dry on manage_exits and exit_market changes nothing.

curl -H "Authorization: Bearer $(gcloud auth print-identity-token)" https://trade-orchestrator-508722.uc.r.appspot.com/cron/<job>
