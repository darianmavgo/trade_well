Market context studies

20-session returns for VOO, IEF, GLD, USO, and HYG, three KMeans fits (k = 2 through 10), GOOGL measured against the five-asset regimes, and a check of whether the oil, gold, and bond clusters predict 5% days. Window for the clusters is 2022-10-24 through 2026-09-25. Written 2026-09-26.

3. GOOGL against those two regimes

Daily close-to-close simple returns, joined to cluster_day. Not the 20-session returns. The clusters were fit on overlapping 20-session returns that already include VOO, so a 20-session regression would recount the same days and use a clustering feature as the benchmark.

Beta is covariance(GOOGL, VOO) / variance(VOO), divisor N. Alpha is 252 × (mean GOOGL − beta × mean VOO) with a zero risk-free rate, the same Jensen residual as the backtest report. The residual column freezes the full-sample beta (1.259) and averages GOOGL − 1.259 × VOO inside each cluster. Re-estimating beta inside a cluster would hide a harder drawdown inside a larger beta, so that residual is the resilience number.

GOOGL's last daily bar in the market database is 2026-09-15, so 8 of the 392 cluster-1 days drop out. 976 days remain. Compounds below multiply only the days inside that cluster. They are not a calendar holding path.

6. Do those clusters predict 5% days?

The ticker list is data/gain_5pct_frequency.db. That file stores totals, not the days, so each 5% move was rebuilt from daily closes: up at least 5%, or down at least 5%. GLD and USO are left out because they define the clusters. 69 of the remaining tickers have bars inside the cluster window. The prediction uses yesterday's cluster. The same-day rates are only the coincidence, because the cluster is not known until that session's closes.

The prior cluster does not predict the next session. Up-day lift is 1.03 and 0.96. Down-day lift is 1.01 and 0.99. The same-day up-day rate is a little higher in cluster 0 (8.33% versus 7.06%), and the down-day rate does not move. Lift near 1 means the regime is not separating 5% days from ordinary days for this ticker list.
